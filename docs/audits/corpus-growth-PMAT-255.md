percentage of sovereign units using at least one family of the theme, minus

## The seven safety exclusions (never appended, never executed)

`bashrs corpus run` executes every Bash entry's transpiled output
(`timeout 2 sh -c` and `dash -c`) with the invoking process's cwd, HOME and
PATH. These seven generated Bash entries would run a command with side
effects or invoke a third-party CLI, so they were routed to
`docs/audits/corpus-pending-PMAT-255.jsonl` and never appended to the
corpus:

| Name | Lane | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `make-gnu-bash-build-j` | 2 | runs make against the runner's cwd under B3 |
| `make-gnu-bash-var-override` | 2 | runs make against the runner's cwd under B3 |
| `make-gnu-bash-recursive-env` | 2 | runs make against the runner's cwd under B3 |
| `make-gnu-bash-dry-run-check` | 2 | runs make against the runner's cwd under B3 |
| `make-gnu-bash-keep-going-ci` | 2 | runs make against the runner's cwd under B3 |
| `polyglot-inline-bash-uv-python` | 3 | uv may download an interpreter and write caches under B3 |
| `agentic-ops-agent-cli-probes` | 4 | invokes a third-party agent CLI under B3 |

## Measured failures (found by the sandboxed run, then moved to pending)

The first sandboxed run of the 233 candidate entries (240 generated minus
the 7 unsafe entries above) reported 18,411 total / 18,409 passed / 2
failed at the top level, but per-entry results showed 8 of the 233
candidates failing at least one of the dimensions this ticket treats as
disqualifying (`transpiled`, `output_contains`, `lint_clean`,
`deterministic`, `output_behavioral`, `cross_shell_agree`,
`metamorphic_consistent`). All 8 were moved to pending with the failing
dimensions as their reason, the remaining 225 ids were renumbered
contiguous, and the corpus was rebuilt and re-run clean (see below).

| Assigned id (before removal) | Name | Failing dimension(s) |
|---|---|---|
| B-16941 | `cron-sched-bash-idempotent-install` | transpiled, output_contains, lint_clean, deterministic, output_behavioral, cross_shell_agree, metamorphic_consistent |
| B-17010 | `polyglot-inline-bash-python-argv` | metamorphic_consistent |
| B-17012 | `polyglot-inline-bash-perl-ne` | metamorphic_consistent |
| B-17017 | `polyglot-inline-bash-yq-yaml` | lint_clean |
| M-17030 | `polyglot-inline-make-oneshell-python` | output_contains |
| M-17037 | `polyglot-inline-make-oneshell-cat` | output_contains |
| M-17041 | `polyglot-inline-make-heredoc-auto-indent` | output_contains |
| B-17147 | `text-pipe-bash-curl-jq-print` | transpiled, output_contains, lint_clean, deterministic, output_behavioral, cross_shell_agree, metamorphic_consistent |

`B-16941` and `B-17147` failed the transpiler's own OR/pipe-in-string-literal
validation (e.g. `crontab -l 2>/dev/null | grep -qF '# raid-sweep' |'` and
`curl -s https://api.github.com/repos/x | jq -r .na'` — both artifacts of
the generator splitting a shell pipeline across a Rust string literal
boundary) and so failed every dimension that depends on a successful
transpile. The other six failed a single dimension each: three
`polyglot-inline-make-*` heredoc/oneshell entries failed `output_contains`
(the pinned `expected_output` did not appear in the purified Makefile
output), and three Bash entries failed `metamorphic_consistent` or
`lint_clean` under re-transpilation.
